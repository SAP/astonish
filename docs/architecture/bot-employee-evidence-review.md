# Automatic employee evidence review

New first-claimed Bot runs pin `employee_review_policy=evidence_bound_v1` in private work JSON. Existing `agent_led_v1` runs without this marker keep their historical validation/read contract; continuations do not retrofit the marker. Generic work updates cannot change it.

`runBotAgentWork` captures the ordinary final candidate, then `reviewBotEmployeeResult` invokes the already-budgeted owner model through the existing tool-free `reviewBotCandidate` / `botContentInput` protocol. The reviewer receives the complete pinned assignment, charter, goals and attachment text; every publishable line and artifact body; actual captured source responses, receipts, and exact digest-bound delegated journal bodies. The assignment umbrella criterion is server-generated without a classifier call; its review explicitly requires assessment of every original subrequirement. Model summaries and source transport success are not factual evidence. Existing content coverage, exact-quote validation and unsupported-claim downgrades remain enforced.

Review happens before immutable publication. A pass is bound to the current candidate content digest; new-policy finished work must have a valid all-satisfied review. Commit validation also prevents publishing an unaccepted candidate through partial status or an agent envelope. This is bounded model judgment, not factual truth certification or owner approval.

Failed/unverified review permits at most two reserved repair attempts. Legacy responses without `repair_kind` retain tool-free text revision and independent re-review. Typed `research` feedback requires explicit missing dimensions, source needs and bounded public query guidance derived from the original assignment. The original producer is never replayed. `api.WithBotEmployeeResearchRunner` is a trusted composition hook: its runner must construct a fresh ordinary producer with the passed narrowed policy and `PolicyRuntime`, retain the same run/session, budget, mandate and research episode, expose only the effective `perplexity_web_search` logical configured route and `web_fetch`, and install the passed verified-read-only classifier on every dispatch path. Grants are dropped, approvals disabled, and unknown/write/send/control effects rejected. No binding means a withheld incomplete draft, not an unrestricted fallback. **Production factory binding and the configured-search→detail→accepted repaired report journey remain unverified in this change.**

A typed `formatting` response can return `formatted_text` and a complete `formatted_review` in the same model response. Formatting is conservatively limited to whitespace/emphasis changes with unchanged normalized claim digest and exact opaque URL identities. The complete original review is validated first; the formatted review is then rebound to the exact formatted candidate digest and run through the same identity, criterion, citation and content-coverage validators and accepted-review gate. Missing assessments or changed facts/citations withhold; no second reviewer or research call is made. This intentionally does not yet accept general paraphrase, reordered sections or table-layout transformations.

Review unavailable, evidence identity/size failure, exhausted budget or exhausted repairs withholds the draft and artifacts, records a blocked/partial outcome, and publishes a runtime limitation without claiming the assignment succeeded. Original receipts remain retained. Repair count alone does not downgrade an adequate accepted answer; captured unmet obligations and limitations still require partial status.

## Original scope, not exhaustive inferred coverage

A broad owner title such as `AI News` requires a useful relevant source-grounded briefing, not an exhaustive survey of every major provider, market, regulatory or other domain. Employee-selected working scope and retained outlines are revisable assumptions, not immutable owner obligations. Missing explicit owner requirements or materially inadequate content still fail; token filler and an empty limitation-only response do not constitute a briefing. Unavailable sources do not invalidate supported useful selective coverage with candid boundaries. Every explicit freshness, source, format and citation requirement remains intact, as do exact-quote checks and unsupported-claim downgrades. Deterministic production-review tests exercise useful multi-source selected coverage, unsupported revenue claims, empty limitations and invented quotations; they do not establish live model adherence.

## Content limitations are not uncertain external actions

`BotReport.Unverified` contains unmet coverage and content requirements as well as operational gaps; its strings must not be interpreted as evidence of a send or write. Runtime adapters assign `effect_state` from classified original calls, observed responses, guard nonexecution and trusted tool outcomes: `read_only` means all dispatched calls were confirmed reads/nonexecution, `confirmed` means effect outcomes were independently accounted for, and `uncertain` means an action may have occurred without a confirmed outcome. Missing/unknown state stays conservative when unresolved outcomes exist. A confirmed effect does not certify the answer's research quality, and a failed source read does not imply a duplicate delivery risk.

Trusted review code may call `SetBotEmployeeReviewLimitation` after persisting blocked work. The retained `review_limitation` contains the exact owner-facing gap, bound to Bot/run/work revision/candidate digest and `Unresolved`; it is not the rejected draft or raw diagnostics. The composer and immutable finalizer share `BotEmployeeOutcomeReason`. For accounted-for effects they publish that limitation under `research_inadequate` without technical narration, observation counts, destination checks or duplicate-action warnings. Real uncertain effects (including unknown effect receipts) retain `agent_effects_unverified` and the existing destination/no-replay warning, even if a useful content limitation exists. No extra model call, topic-keyword filler, implicit retry, artifact acceptance, or relaxed success gate is introduced. Publication integrity binds these typed facts in the report source digest; commit checks the retained limitation against the scoped private work snapshot.

Adapter integration must populate these fields from actual runtime receipts, never infer read-only execution from an empty `Changed` list or reviewer prose. A narrowly scoped exception is a successful final **text-only** directed research continuation: the server-validated narrowed policy marker must still match the request/context, with a live guard, verified-read classifier, no approval/resume path, zero dispatched calls, zero tool responses, empty journal, no errors/blocking/pending controls/artifacts/effect outputs/unverified receipts and an identified nonempty final model answer. That attempt-local journal can attest `read_only` nonexecution; it does not inherit the original producer's state or certify factual adequacy. Ordinary empty attempts, arbitrary custom-runner unknown results, missing responses, orphan responses and failed completions remain unknown/uncertain, preserving no-replay behavior. Old persisted reports retain their conservative behavior until fresh runtime facts exist.

Review attempts and text-repair attempts are reserved in fenced private work JSON before model invocation (`employee_review_attempts`, `employee_review_pending`, `employee_repair_pending`, and `repairs`). Pending/retained candidates cannot restart the ordinary producer. Producer text is held at the adapter sink; a subsequent tool boundary releases interim narration, while the last text and producer `done` remain withheld for committed report publication.

A directed research producer shares the run's evidence journal service. Its model boundaries can advance the private work revision by retaining new tool responses even if its final completion fails. Review reloads the current **leased** work immediately after that producer returns, before either withholding a limited/failed continuation or replacing its candidate. This is not a CAS retry, lease repair, or acceptance of failed prose: failed research still publishes only the safe outcome selected from retained limitation and effect receipts, retains the prior private candidate and new evidence, and never replays the producer. Fencing or cancellation that prevents the reload still prevents any late publication. The deterministic production-factory/SQLite journey includes a final continuation error after configured search and decisive-source captures, proving the limitation can commit rather than failing on a stale pre-research revision.

## Validation diagnostics and bounded correction

`botWorkJSON` routes both `*botWorkContentReview` and the embedded review in `*botEmployeeAssessment` through the same identity/criterion and content-protocol validation. Previously the employee destination bypassed citation correction because a concrete Go pointer assertion recognized only the legacy review type. Both now share the existing **two model-call total** allowance for strict JSON/schema or citation-protocol correction. The original input, guided response schema and complete prior response remain unchanged/labeled untrusted; independently valid negative content and criterion findings are retained. Candidate identity failures cannot receive correction. A repeated malformed citation or missing coverage after correction fails closed. No assessment is dropped, no arbitrary source substituted, and no producer/tool/effect is replayed. Formatting review still validates separately after exact claim preservation.

Private structured logs tagged `[bot-review-validation-v1]` record server-typed `stage`, allowlisted `reason`, one-based model `attempt` (zero before/after the shared JSON call), and whether the existing correction slot is being used. Stages distinguish boundary, input, response contract, model response, completion, strict decode, candidate identity/criteria, content protocol, repair feedback and formatted candidate. Reasons distinguish invalid JSON/schema and content protocol coverage/ref/quote/verdict/explanation failures; arbitrary provider/store errors remain `rejected` unless a known cancellation/fence/forbidden sentinel applies. Underlying error messages, response text, URLs, quotes, item/ref hashes and decoder field names are never logged. Owner-facing withholding text is unchanged. Completed model transport is not proof of valid review.
